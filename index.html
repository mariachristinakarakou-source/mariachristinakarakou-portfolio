MIT Critical Data x NSRI · 2026

Advancing Machine Learning Applications in Critical Healthcare Datasets

Developed a comprehensive 14-page benchmarking study evaluating the structural identifiability of mathematical frameworks in microbial growth dynamics. By conducting a high-resolution comparative analysis between the Modified Gompertz and Logistic models, I investigated the impact of model symmetry on kinetic parameter estimation. Utilizing Non-linear Least Squares (NLLS) regression and Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) weights, I quantified a 12-15% systemic bias in traditional phenomenological models during the lag-phase transition. The entire manuscript was architected in LaTeX/Overleaf, adhering to professional arXiv publication standards to ensure rigorous data visualization and technical clarity. This project involved synthesizing complex biophysical datasets (OD600) and applying advanced statistical methodologies to optimize bioprocess scaling and predictive fidelity.
